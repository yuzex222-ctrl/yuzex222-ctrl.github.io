I am an undergraduate studying Honors Mathematics and Honors Computer Science at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science and machine learning theory.

I work with [Nikhil Bansal](https://bansal.engin.umich.edu/) on determinant maximization and optimal experimental design, [Thatchaphol Saranurak](https://sites.google.com/site/thsaranurak/) on graph algorithms, and [Zhenyu Liao](https://zhenyu-liao.github.io/) on neural network training dynamics.

## Research interests

- Approximation algorithms and combinatorial optimization
- Fast graph algorithms and parameterized algorithms
- Mathematical foundations of machine learning
